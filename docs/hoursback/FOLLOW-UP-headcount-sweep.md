# Follow-up: the team-page sweep never ran properly

**Raised by Russ, 2026-08-26.** The first business he opened — BeginRight
Employment — has a Team page under its About tab, and the record still says
team size unknown.

## What went wrong

The website reader visits at most five pages per site, and only counts people
on a page whose address contains team / staff / about / meet. On most sites the
team page was never among the five it opened. Result: **2,010 of 2,044
businesses carry no headcount at all**, and the 34 that do include national
totals for local branches (Echo Global Logistics 800, Keller Williams 400).

## What has to happen

1. A targeted pass that goes looking for the team page specifically — follows
   the About tab, the nav menu, and the obvious addresses — rather than hoping
   it lands in the first five pages.
2. Read the people too, not just the count: names and roles feed the interview
   (who to ask for) and the outreach (who to address).
3. Treat a multi-location national brand as unknown rather than trusting its
   company-wide number.
4. Fill whatever is left with the trade-based estimate, clearly marked as an
   estimate and never quoted as fact.

## Why it matters now

Team size no longer sets the price — every business is $999 for five hours. But
it still decides who Russ asks for on the call, how long the discovery
interview runs, and how believable the audit is. And the people on a team page
are the named individuals the guarantee gets counted against.
